# Suitability and Appropriateness Policy

## Purpose

Ensures investment recommendations and discretionary mandates at Helvetia Private Bank AG align with the client’s knowledge, experience, objectives, and risk capacity.

## Questionnaires

A suitability profile must be completed before:

- Opening a discretionary mandate
- Recommending complex or illiquid instruments
- Materially changing strategy or risk budget

Appropriateness checks apply when the client initiates transactions in complex products on an execution-only basis.

## Incomplete or outdated profiles

If the suitability questionnaire is missing, expired, or marked incomplete, the RM must not issue new recommendations. Existing discretionary mandates may continue under prior parameters only until the profile is refreshed, unless Compliance requires a pause.

## Documentation

Recommendations must reference the current suitability profile version and note any client deviations that were explicitly accepted in writing.
