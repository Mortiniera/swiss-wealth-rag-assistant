# Complaint Handling Procedure

## Purpose

Defines how Helvetia Private Bank AG records, investigates, and closes client complaints.

## Intake

Any oral or written expression of dissatisfaction about a product, service, fee, delay, or employee conduct is logged as a service request of type **complaint** within one business day.

## Investigation

1. Acknowledge receipt to the client within two business days.
2. Assign an owner (usually client service; RM remains informed).
3. Gather account facts, prior interactions, and relevant policies.
4. Propose a resolution or escalation within ten business days unless complexity requires an extension logged on the ticket.

## Unresolved complaints

Complaints open beyond the SLA without an approved extension are marked **sla_breach**. Escalation follows **POL-ESC-001**.

## Outcomes

Closures must record outcome category (upheld, partially upheld, not upheld, withdrawn) and a factual summary. Do not promise compensation outside approved authority.
