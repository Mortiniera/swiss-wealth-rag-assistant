# Escalation Matrix

## Purpose

Specifies when Helvetia Private Bank AG employees must escalate operational or compliance issues beyond the first-line owner.

## Matrix

| Situation | First line | Escalate to | Timing |
| --------- | ---------- | ----------- | ------ |
| KYC documents expired blocking a transfer | RM / client service | Compliance | Same day if client impact |
| Account restriction unexplained to RM | Operations | Compliance | Within 1 business day |
| Complaint SLA breach | Client service | Operations lead | Immediately on breach flag |
| Suspected unusual activity | RM / client service | Compliance (AML path) | Immediate |
| Cross-border communication uncertainty | RM | Compliance | Before outbound advisory content |
| Missing suitability profile before recommendation | RM | Advisory desk / Compliance | Before any recommendation |

## How to escalate

1. Update the related service request with facts and impact.
2. Notify the escalation role via the internal channel recorded on the ticket.
3. Do not wait for client pressure to document the escalation.

## After escalation

First-line owners remain responsible for client communication cadence unless Compliance instructs a communication freeze.
