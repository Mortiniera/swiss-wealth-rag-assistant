# Client Communication Standards

## Purpose

Sets baseline expectations for client-facing communication by Helvetia Private Bank AG employees.

## Channels

Use only channels recorded in the client’s communication preferences (email, phone, postal, or secure portal). Do not use personal messaging apps for banking content.

## Tone and content

- Be factual, clear, and proportional to the client’s language preference.
- Do not provide personal tax or legal advice; refer to external advisers when needed.
- Do not disclose other clients’ information or internal investigation details.

## Restrictions

If a communication restriction is active (for example cross-border limits under **POL-XBR-001**), outbound marketing and certain advisory content may be blocked. Operational notices about account status remain allowed when required for service continuity.

## Record keeping

Material calls and emails must be summarised as interactions in the banking domain record.
