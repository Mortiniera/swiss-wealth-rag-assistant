# Cross-Border Communication Restrictions

## Purpose

Limits certain outbound communications and solicitations when a Helvetia Private Bank AG client is resident in, or temporarily located in, a jurisdiction with marketing or advisory restrictions.

## Restricted activities

When a cross-border restriction flag is active on the client or household:

- Do not send unsolicited product offers or strategy proposals
- Do not schedule onboarding calls while the client is in a restricted location without Compliance clearance
- Limit contact to servicing existing contractual obligations and regulatory notices

## Allowed activities

- Responding to client-initiated service requests about existing accounts
- Providing statements, tax documents, and operational status updates
- Escalating urgent risk or fraud matters

## RM checklist

Before outbound advisory content, confirm domicile, travel flags, and communication preferences. When unsure, route to Compliance rather than guessing.
