# Client Onboarding Procedure

## Purpose

This procedure defines how Helvetia Private Bank AG opens a new private-banking relationship for a prospective client. It applies to discretionary and advisory mandates booked in Switzerland.

## Scope

Applies to relationship managers (RMs) and client-service officers preparing onboarding packs. Compliance viewers may inspect completion status for escalation review.

## Required steps

1. **Identity collection** — Collect government ID, proof of address dated within 90 days, and tax residency self-certification.
2. **Source-of-wealth narrative** — Document employment, business ownership, inheritance, or investment proceeds with supporting evidence proportional to expected assets.
3. **Risk and product assessment** — Complete suitability and appropriateness questionnaires before any investment recommendation.
4. **Account setup** — Open at least one custody account; record communication preferences and any early restrictions.
5. **Four-eyes check** — A second authorised employee confirms the pack is complete before the account is marked active.

## Incomplete onboarding

If any mandatory document is missing, the relationship remains in **onboarding_incomplete** status. No discretionary trading instructions may be accepted until the pack is complete or a documented waiver is approved by Compliance.

## Escalation

Escalate to Compliance when identity documents appear inconsistent, source-of-wealth explanations are unsupported, or the client refuses required tax certifications.
