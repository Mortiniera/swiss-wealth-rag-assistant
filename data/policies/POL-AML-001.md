# AML Escalation Procedure

## Purpose

Sets the internal path for escalating unusual activity or suspected money-laundering indicators to Compliance at Helvetia Private Bank AG.

## Indicators requiring review

Examples (non-exhaustive):

- Sudden change in transaction size, frequency, or destination relative to the known profile
- Use of multiple accounts to fragment transfers without economic rationale
- Reluctance to provide source-of-funds evidence for large cash or crypto-linked inflows
- Activity involving jurisdictions on the bank’s heightened-monitoring list

## Escalation path

1. Employee documents facts in an interaction note (no speculative conclusions in client-facing text).
2. Employee opens or updates a service request of type **aml_review**.
3. Case is routed to Compliance for decision: clear, enhanced monitoring, or formal internal report.
4. Until Compliance decides, the RM must not tip off the client about the suspicion itself.

## Prohibited behaviour

Employees must not alter, delete, or backdate transaction narratives to avoid escalation. Tipping-off about a formal investigation is prohibited.

## Confidentiality

This procedure is classified **confidential**. Do not share externally or with unauthorised roles.
